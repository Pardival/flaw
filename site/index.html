The First Morning

She opened the shutters and the sea was there, perfectly still, as if it were waiting for someone.

The sand had covered the last step during the night, and the house smelled of salt and old wood.

“Are you staying?” he asked from the kitchen.

She did not answer right away. Outside, a gull circled above the pines, and something in her, something she could not name, circled with it.

Flaw in one minute

A real page

Trade paperback, pocket, A4 or US Letter: full pages, page numbers, automatic French typography.

Four ways to write

Novel with chapters, screenplay in standard format, structured document, or blank page.

Nothing but the text

Menus fade away while you write. Focus mode keeps only the last few lines.

Import and export

Import Word, PDF, Markdown or text. Export print-ready PDF, Word, EPUB or Fountain. Interface in six languages.

Images and covers

Add and crop images, and design your book's front and back cover.

Yours, and only yours

No account, no cloud, no ads. Your texts stay on your computer, with one-click backups.

First launch

Flaw is a free project without a paid certificate: your system will ask for confirmation the first time.

Mac

  1. Open Flaw-mac.dmg and drag Flaw into Applications.
  2. Launch Flaw. If macOS refuses, open System Settings › Privacy & Security.
  3. Click “Open Anyway”, then confirm.

Windows

  1. Run Flaw-windows.exe.
  2. If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, click “More info”.
  3. Click “Run anyway”. Flaw installs for you only, without admin rights.
